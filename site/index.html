Careful by design.

An app that deletes files has one job before all others: delete only the right ones.

Stays inside the lines.

Every item has a home folder it must sit inside. Links that point anywhere else are refused before anything is touched.

Claude remembers.

Your memory, settings, skills, agents, and credentials are off limits, checked on every single path.

Undo for your files.

Only data that rebuilds itself is deleted outright. Your own files go to the Trash, and the Trash is never emptied unless you ask.

Every clean, written down.

One line per item in ~/Library/Logs/Neuralyzer/clean.log: when, what, and how much.

Ready in a minute.
